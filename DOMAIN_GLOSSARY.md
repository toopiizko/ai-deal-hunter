# Domain Glossary

- **Listing**: A specific second-hand sale advertisement.
- **Product**: The normalized item represented by one or more listings.
- **Deal Score**: A 0–100 estimate of value, condition, seller trust, freshness, and risk.
- **Fair Price**: Estimated reasonable price for the product configuration.
- **Market Price**: Reference average observed price.
- **Risk Score**: A separate 0–100 scam or transaction-risk indicator.
- **Watchlist**: Locally saved listings a user wants to revisit.
- **Price Alert**: Local prototype criteria such as a maximum price or minimum Deal Score.
- **Source**: Original marketplace hosting a listing.
- **Seller**: Person or account offering a listing.
